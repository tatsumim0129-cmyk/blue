The Hidden Gem of Kapolei

Known affectionately by locals as the ultimate rooftop escape, Cafe Blue Kapolei (CBK) delivers an exceptional chef-driven cafeteria experience open to the general public.

Nestled on the fourth floor of the Wakea Wing, our spacious air-conditioned indoor dining room features expansive floor-to-ceiling windows, while our scenic outdoor patio boasts breezy tables covered in signature blue umbrellas with gorgeous views of the burgeoning Kapolei landscape.

Island Atmosphere

Whether you need a quick morning pick-me-up or a relaxing midday lunch break, enjoy full wheelchair accessibility, friendly service, and a beautiful space to connect.
